§1

The system

  • Environment: custom C orbital-mechanics environment (PufferLib Ocean pattern) — analytic Kepler propagation, impulsive burns with Tsiolkovsky fuel accounting (478 m/s budget), 60 s decision epochs with time-warp actions, terminal success boxes down to 5 km / 1 m/s. 2D and 3D (classical elements; inclination/RAAN with singularity-free consumers). Secular-J2 propagation merged and validated, default-off (§7).
  • Policy: PPO (PufferLib), discrete 16/20/30-action heads (tangential/radial/normal burns at three magnitudes, combined burns, warps), curriculum-trained with potential-based shaping (γ=1 exact telescoping — provably policy-invariant).
  • Navigation stack: worker-side estimation layer with zero C changes — batched range+bearing EKF and a bearings-only modified-polar filter with analytic STM; CRLB-conditioned acquisition surrogate at training, real batch initial-orbit-determination at evaluation (grid + multi-start Gauss-Newton, geodesic angle residual, 3-axis velocity lattice).
  • Embedded port: the trained policy re-implemented in allocation-free C — 100.0000% action agreement over 7,048 decision vectors, closed-loop action stream md5-identical to PyTorch, p50 50.5 µs/decision single-core.
  • Baselines: Lambert two-impulse targeting (learned policy: 1.14× Lambert Δv time-matched) and a scripted GNC expert (99.2% on the headline task).

§3

The attribution ladder — why the nav result is believable

"It works on estimates" is easy to claim and easy to get wrong. Every navigation result here ships inside a three-arm design: a floor (truth-trained policy flown blind, zero-shot), a control (trained on range+bearing estimates — fully observable, so maneuvers buy the estimator nothing), and the treatment (trained on bearings-only estimates, where observability structure exists to learn). All arms share one warm-start file and paired eval scenarios.

Treatment — trained bearings-only98.0% (98.0–98.5 × 3 seeds)= own mode99.5%

Three things this table proves that a single number cannot:

  • Estimates-per-se teach nothing — at the loose box the control flown blind lands at the floor (60.5% vs 58.0%).
  • At tight tolerances they actively harm: the control collapses to 13–18% flown blind — below the untrained floor. Training on always-reliable estimates unlearns blind-window caution. This negative-transfer result replicates at both tight boxes.
  • Only observability structure during training produces the capability — and it costs nothing: the treatment's truth-mode score (99.5%) exceeds its parent's (97.0%).

The same design was run first in 2D (five arms, including an uncertainty-channel ablation and an action-availability ablation) with a pre-registered counterfactual information metric — §5 covers what it found.

§4

The verification story

The most important fact about this project is that its worst bug was found by its own tooling, disclosed, and used to rebuild better — not discovered by a reviewer.

2026-08-10

Independent-oracle audit finds a sign inversion in a true-anomaly↔mean-anomaly conversion: every burn had been silently teleporting orbital phase (~24.5°/episode of free drift). The canonical checkpoint fell from 97.5% to 13% under corrected dynamics. Every pre-fix number and checkpoint was retired the same day.

recovery

Rebuilt from the corrected baseline with a fix stack chosen by controlled experiment (policy-invariant shaping at γ=1, physically-defined initial conditions, corrected observations), reaching 100.0% × 5 seeds on the headline task — better than the buggy-era peak, on honest physics. A scripted GNC expert (99.2%) and Lambert baseline bound the task from below and above.

since then

Every C change ships with bitwise regression anchors — three canonical trajectories whose action-stream md5s must not move (they've caught real drift twice). Metric code is mutation-tested: 14 seeded errors, 0 escaped. Eleven historical "the metric measured something different than the implementation did" bugs drove this discipline.

red-team first

Adversarial review gates implementation. The 3D-nav extension was red-teamed before a line was written: 4 blockers found, including a filter-chart pole that was exactly gimbal-locked in the design's own regression arm, and 7 truth-leak channels in the proposed observation layout that no existing test would have caught. A leak-check gate (garbage the estimate, assert every estimated channel moves and no truth channel does) now runs in CI — validated against a deliberately-leaky encode.

controls everywhere

No capability claim without its null. Floors, negative controls, cross-mode evals, paired scenarios, multi-seed spreads reported as ranges (a 10-episode smoke's regression coefficient of +0.44 died at 200 episodes — reported as such). The J2 module shipped with its validation suite pinned to published precession rates and its own spec's five errors documented.

§5

Findings a navigation shop would care about

Dual control is affirmatively absent — and that's the physically correct policy

The 2D five-arm experiment pre-registered a counterfactual metric: at every decision, the exact Fisher information a probe burn would buy, regressed against whether the policy actually burned. Result: null after covariate adjustment in every bearings-only arm — and significantly negative unadjusted. The physics agrees: at these ranges the filter gains information faster by coasting (Δlog₁₀ tr(P) −0.50 per 3 decisions) than through a burn (−0.31), because maneuvers reset drift-information accumulation. An agent that probed would waste fuel and information; the trained agent correctly does not.

What it learns instead: passive information discipline

Truth-trained policies flown blind burn 1.75× harder during the blind window, spending ~130 m/s before first acquisition. Estimate-trained policies invert this: 3× burn suppression while blind, median zero Δv before acquisition, then decisive action on a converged estimate. Every 3D episode acquires at exactly the 46-minute sim-time acquisition floor.

At the tight box, navigation is over-solved — guidance binds

Inside the 5 km box the bearings-only filter's velocity error is 0.001 m/s (p90 0.005) against a 1 m/s tolerance — three orders of magnitude of margin. The binding constraint is guidance behavior under the estimate stream, not estimate quality. This settled a design-study prediction ("the 3D dividend lands on velocity information") by measurement: there was no velocity-information deficit left to buy back.

The plane channel is not an independent information channel

Recon measured 3D plane error as re-projected range ambiguity (correlation → 1.000) — which killed a planned plane-error acceptance gate before it could certify garbage, the same failure mode that made the early IOD gates accept multi-1000-km epoch errors (a stated, still-true caveat: "acquired" is a tracking claim, not a precision claim).

§6

Claim boundaries — read these as part of the results

Scope limits, stated plainly
  • Two-body dynamics for all §2 results. Secular J2 is merged and validated but no policy has trained under it yet (§7). No drag, no third body, no SRP.
  • Navigation lineage is e ≤ 0.05 today. Guidance-only lineages reach e ≤ 0.30 (and e ≤ 0.50 at MEO in 2D); the bearings-only stack has not yet been proven beyond near-circular — the e-ladder now running is exactly this test.
  • Impulsive burns, 60 s epochs, single deputy, no debris field in the current scenario set.
  • IOD acceptance gates certify convergence, not precision — worst-case epoch error at acceptance can reach hundreds of km; latency and success numbers are honest, the "acquired" label is not a precision claim.
  • J2 rungs will claim mean elements only: the mean-vs-osculating gap at LEO (+5.6 km in semi-major axis) exceeds the tight box, so tight-box fidelity under J2 requires a conversion layer that does not exist yet.
  • Single machine, CPU PPO. Results are wall-clock-honest (50M steps ≈ 1.5–2 h per arm) but no distributed-training claims are made.

§8

Likely questions, answered the way the data answers them

Why RL at all — classical GNC solves rendezvous?
It does, and the project keeps a scripted GNC expert (99.2%) and Lambert baseline (learned policy at 1.14× Lambert Δv, time-matched) precisely to stay honest about that. What the learned policy adds is unified guidance under partial observability: the same network that plans multi-burn transfers also learned blind-window discipline and estimate-converged action timing — behaviors that classical stacks implement as hand-tuned mode logic. The attribution ladder shows that skill is real, separable, and trainable.
How do you know the physics is right?
Because it was once wrong and the project caught it: an independent universal-variable oracle (validated 56/56 against published test cases) disagreed with the environment, the disagreement traced to a sign inversion, and everything downstream was retired and rebuilt. The oracle, bitwise anchors, and mutation-tested metrics have gated every change since.
What would break first in the real world?
The measurement model: ideal bearings at fixed cadence, no sensor outages, no attitude coupling, no maneuver-execution error. The claim boundaries in §6 are ordered roughly by how fast each would bite. The architecture keeps the estimation layer worker-side and swappable specifically so measurement realism can be added without touching the verified dynamics core.
What's the single result you'd defend under hostile review?
The negative-transfer control: policies trained on always-reliable estimates score below an untrained policy when flown blind (13% vs 46% at the tight box, replicated at a second box). It's the sharpest evidence that observability structure during training — not exposure to estimates — is what produces estimate-robust behavior, and it has direct implications for how one should train flight software on simulated navigation.
