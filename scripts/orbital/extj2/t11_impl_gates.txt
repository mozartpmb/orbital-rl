================================================================================
 ADDENDUM — THE SMOKE FOUND A SECOND BLOCKER: THE DAY-WARP IS UNAFFORDABLE
                                IN NAV MODE AT nav_max_ticks=0
================================================================================

The first 2M smoke ran 15 minutes and reported Epoch 0 / Steps 0. Nothing had
crashed and no metric was wrong; it was simply not going to finish.

MECHANISM. `orbital_nav._nav_step` sub-steps the filter once per minute of tau
when nav_max_ticks=0. Row 30 is tau=1440, so ONE day-warp decision is 1440 EKF
updates -- 4x the previous worst row (tau=360) and 24x the median. Under a
uniform policy the mean cost per decision goes

    rows 0-29 (pre-T11)    22.0 ticks/decision   max  360
    rows 0-30 (T11)        67.7 ticks/decision   max 1440

MEASURED THROUGHPUT, T11 mixture, bearings_only, 8 envs (box under load ~50, so
read the RATIOS, not the absolutes):

    rows 0-29, K=0      22.5 env-steps/s     the pre-T11 baseline
    rows 0-30, K=0       4.4 env-steps/s     5.1x slower
    rows 0-30, K=120    19.8 env-steps/s     0.88x of baseline

Calibrated against a real trainer -- j2nav T-J2BO-nav logged 2.2K SPS on
bearings-only, 49.9M steps in 6 h -- that is

    rung A  50M    K=0 ~32 h      K=120 ~7 h
    rung B 200M    K=0 ~129 h     K=120 ~29 h      (5.4 DAYS vs ~1.2)

WHY A CAP IS SAFE HERE. MAJOR-7 replaced the broken "cap tau but tick at 60 s"
form -- which pinned the last sub-tick to the wrong epoch and diverged the
filter -- with a fixed COUNT and an adaptive INTERVAL: n=min(tau,K),
dt=tau*60/n. Filter and truth stay on one clock at every K. So this is the
supported path, not a workaround.

DIVERGENCE vs K, measured on a PURE-DAY-WARP policy (the worst case that
exists), W1_driftwait, 8 envs x 40 decisions:

    K      dt between fixes    diverge rate    wall
    0            1 min            0.000       209.4 s
    30          48 min            0.109         4.8 s
    60          24 min            0.041         9.4 s
    120         12 min            0.003        18.1 s
    240          6 min            0.003        36.9 s

120 is the knee: it TIES the uncapped filter and K=240 at 0.003, 36x better
than K=30, for 11.6x the speed of uncapped.

COST TO THE WARM-START ROOTS: NONE MEASURED. The roots trained at K=0, and
K=120 re-times rows 16/17 (tau=180/360) as well as row 30 -- 28 of 31 rows are
untouched. j2bonav-wide zero-shot on E0_j2, 40 eps:

    K=0     36/40 = 90.0%   dv 274.5 m/s   efficiency 1.074x   207 s
    K=120   36/40 = 90.0%   dv 274.5 m/s   efficiency 1.083x    92 s

Same success, same fuel, 2.25x the speed. (The action-stream md5 differs, as it
must -- the filter is on a different tick schedule -- so this is a capability
equivalence, not a bit-identity claim.)
