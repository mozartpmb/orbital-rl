## 1. VERDICT on the T13 design

### Supported — do not touch

- **Warm from the tight-skill root.** Strongest-supported choice in the plan. Converges from four directions: the project's own 5x-replicated bootstrap law; Ray Interference's f_k(0)=0 saddle (Schaul 2019 — a zero-performance component generates no reward-bearing data and cannot leave the saddle in-mixture); the RFT continual-learning hard-sample result (arXiv:2507.05386 — no-positive-reward tasks degrade, not learn); Wolczyk ICML 2024. No agent dissents.
- **LR 1e-2 → 1e-3.** Highest-leverage single change per Mirzadeh NeurIPS 2020 (later-task LR is the #1 forgetting knob), Ahmed ICML 2019 (a converged low-entropy policy sits in a sharp landscape where 1e-2 is effectively a much larger step — the mechanistic post-mortem of both seesaw swings), and the CS231n 10x fine-tune heuristic. The campaign doc's own reading ("both swings ran at acquisition LR") is the same diagnosis.
- **Dead-cell (W1_driftwait) exclusion.** Correct and over-determined: incompetent root + unvalidated acquisition surrogate at day-warp arcs (campaign doc). No literature needed.
- **tight=0.25.** Right order of magnitude for *defending* a present skill — Ibrahim 2024's strong-shift case needed 25% replay, and your 10%-at-1e-2 failure is consistent with under-weighted + over-plastic. Keep it.

### Contradicted — but only one item crosses the "change a prepped run" bar

- **The single mid-run eval at ~50M is the one outright-failure-shaped flaw.** Every angle independently hit this. Policy churn drifts in tens-to-hundreds of updates (Schaul 2022); both prior collapses *completed* within 50M; PPO has no replay buffer, so per Nikishin 2022 there is no recovery channel once tight crosses the no-successes threshold — at which point the project's own law says the remaining ~50-90M steps are spent, unrecoverably, feeding wide-cell drift. A midpoint snapshot brackets a collapse; it cannot prevent or localize one. **Change required: per-cell probes (20-50 eps/cell) every ~10M, tight-cell rolling success as a live training metric, per-cell-best checkpointing, and a pre-registered stop/rollback rule** (e.g., tight rolling success sustained below ~70% of its start for 2-3M steps → pause, eval, roll back to the last tight-good checkpoint). This is telemetry + a watchdog, not a redesign — same pattern as the existing flatline tripwires.
- **LR-only is not a documented complete fix.** Three independent lines (Mirzadeh, Ash & Adams 2020, Ibrahim 2024) show LR reduction lessens forgetting but never eliminates it, and RL's Razor implies what matters is *cumulative* KL drift — 100M at 1e-3 can accumulate what 10M at 1e-2 did. This doesn't predict outright failure, so it doesn't force a loss change tonight — but it does mean T13 is a bet, not a guarantee, and the monitoring above is what makes the bet cheap.
- **Pre-register the most likely "failure" that isn't one:** Ibrahim measured that halving LR measurably *slowed new-task adaptation*; you're dropping 10x while asking E3_j2 to climb from 6.5%. A plausible T13 outcome is **no seesaw but incomplete wide-cell recovery at 100M** — that is an LR stability/plasticity trade playing out as designed, not evidence the mixture is wrong. Decide now that this outcome means "extend/anneal," not "redesign."

### The one contested change worth considering: a tight-cell-only anchor

Every published defense of a minority skill during continued RL pairs data rehearsal with an **output-space anchor** — CLEAR's cloning losses (KL 0.01/value-L2 0.005), Wolczyk's BC (2.0) / kickstarting (0.5, decayed). T13's tight defense is reward-mediated only: under sparse terminal reward, the tight gradient exists *only while tight episodes keep succeeding*. A transient churn dip silences it and re-enters your never-bootstraps regime mid-run. The fix is a small KL/CE to `t11t_tight_child`'s own policy **applied only on tight-cell rollouts** (start 0.01-0.05 relative weight, CLEAR-end since the anchor IS the init) — dense signal that survives reward silence, and it cannot cap wide-cell recovery because it never touches wide-cell minibatches. Critically it must be per-cell: a global anchor to this checkpoint would pin the wide cells to their broken 29/6.5/40 behavior.

**Recommendation:** if this is <1 day of trainer plumbing, add it; it converts T13's single point of failure into a supervised defense and is the best-supported insurance in the entire research packet. If it risks the launch or muddies the bundle further (see attribution risk below), ship T13 as prepped *with the monitoring/rollback watchdog*, and hold the anchor as the first amendment for T13b — the rollback rule bounds the cost of omitting it to lost steps rather than a lost run.

### Accepted costs — flag, don't fix tonight

- **Attribution is confounded**: T13 bundles ≥4 changes (LR, tight weight, deepened long cells, dead-cell exclusion) vs. either failed run. The clean control (re-mix's exact mixture at 1e-3) is what Karpathy/Jones hygiene demands, but the bar for stopping a prepped run isn't met. Accept, and if attribution later matters, note OpenAI Five's Rerun lesson anyway argues the final artifact should be one clean run of whatever recipe wins.
- **Single seed**: given in-project pointiness (seed 1337: 0% where 42: 81.3%), neither a 6/7 nor a seesaw from one seed settles the recipe. Plan a second seed before writing conclusions to memory.
- **Verify before launch, no change needed:** (a) how many PPO epochs per rollout batch — effective trust region is epochs × LR (Engstrom ICLR 2020), and halving epochs is as protective as halving LR; know the number so the outcome is attributable. (b) whether batch advantage normalization is on — with terminal-reward density differing ~7x across 3000-22000-step cells and gamma=1 shaping returns scaling with episode length, batch mean/std normalization silently rescales and sign-shifts the short cells' signal (VAPO, PopArt). Don't change it tonight; log per-cell stats (below) and pre-register: if per-cell advantage/return scales differ >~3x, per-cell normalization is the first amendment.

## 2. CHEAP ADD-ONS (no redesign; ranked by diagnostic value per effort)

1. **Per-cell probe battery + rollback watchdog** (mandatory, see above). 20-50 eps/cell every ~10M; per-cell-best checkpoints; dense checkpointing in the first ~5M steps — the LP-FT distortion mechanism (Kumar ICLR 2022) says the kill window is the earliest updates, when fresh value error on the re-weighted mixture backprops large gradients through the shared trunk. This is plausibly how the re-mix re-zeroed tight.
2. **Per-cell KL(π_t ‖ π_warmstart) on fixed held-out state sets** — computable offline from checkpoints, near-free. RL's Razor: KL drift on a cell's state distribution predicts forgetting on that cell tens of M steps before an eval catches it.
3. **Per-cell entropy + per-update KL logging** (Jones/Schulman). Discriminates the two stories with opposite remedies: KL/entropy churn while tight trains = update-size problem (clamp LR/epochs); calm updates with smooth tight-action drift toward wide modes = data-starvation/interference (mixture/anchor/architecture).
4. **Per-cell advantage & value-target mean/std logging** — the PopArt diagnostic. Tests whether tight's 0.25 *sampling* weight is a much smaller *gradient* share.
5. **Per-cell gradient cosines + norm ratios** inside the PPO update (a few lines) — the PCGrad *diagnostic* only (Kurin/Xin NeurIPS 2022 both say don't adopt the optimizer). Negative tight-vs-wide cosines = directional interference; big norm ratios = scale problem. Different fixes.
6. **Init-time plasticity check** (minutes): dormant-unit fraction and effective feature rank of the 128-d encoder on states from each cell. This lineage has been through multiple 1e-2 ladders; if features are rank-collapsed (Lyle 2022, Dohare Nature 2024), slow wide-cell recovery will masquerade as a mixture failure.
7. **Re-mix autopsy** (uses existing checkpoints, zero training): eval TIGHT_5k1 on the 50M re-mix run's intermediate checkpoints to get the 92.5→0 decay curve. Instant collapse implicates update size/interference; gradual decay implicates starvation. Also re-verify the tight cell was actually sampled/weighted/evaluated as intended in that run — this project's bug history (eval classifier, ϖ gauge, true_to_mean, bug #15) has earned that paranoia for a literal 0/200.
8. **Alpha-sweep interpolation probe** (also §4): pure eval, an afternoon, and if some α holds tight + wide simultaneously it hands you a *better warm start than the prepped one* before T13 even launches.

## 3. DISTILLATION FALLBACK SPEC (ready to hand off if T13 seesaws)

**Headline design decision — the fallback as currently planned (pure supervised phase → optional RL polish) is the configuration the literature and this project both reject.** Kickstarting (Schmitt 2018) found pure distillation plateaus at/below teacher, and the sharp BC→RL handoff is structurally the reward-reshape-on-committed-policy transition that failed 4/4 in R5. Run it **single-phase**: PPO + annealed per-teacher CE, from day one.

- **Student:** fresh Linear(38→128) + LSTM(128) + Discrete(31). Fresh, not warm — sidesteps this lineage's accumulated plasticity damage (Lyle, Ash & Adams); capacity vouched by Rusu (multi-task students at ≤25% teacher size retain >90%) and your own specialists.
- **Teachers / routing:** mode table by cell ID — `t11_generalist_rungB.pt` labels its 5 cells, `t11t_tight_child.pt` labels TIGHT_5k1. **Both teachers simultaneously in one interleaved stream from step one** — sequential (generalist first, tight after) recreates the seesaw inside supervised learning (Progress & Compress needed EWC for exactly that). W1_driftwait stays out (no competent teacher + unvalidated surrogate).
- **State distribution — student-driven, mandatory:** student rolls out in each cell's env; the cell's teacher LSTM runs in lockstep on the *student's* obs stream with its own hidden state (reset at episode reset), teacher probs stored per step in the rollout buffer. Backing: Czarnecki AISTATS 2019 (student-driven ≈3x faster than teacher-driven, ≈10x vs uniform, and covariate shift hits hardest exactly at terminal tight-box approach); DAgger O(T·ε) vs offline BC's O(T²·ε) — at T=3000-22000 offline BC on teacher trajectories is the worst possible regime. LSTM handling is exactly Kickstarting's: no hidden-state matching loss, no stored-state/burn-in machinery needed (that's only for offline replay, R2D2).
- **Loss:** PPO loss (env reward incl. shaping, entropy bonus ON) + λ_k · CE(π_teacher ‖ π_student) per step, **forward CE against the teacher's full 31-way softmax at τ=1**. Not argmax labels, not MSE (Rusu Table 1: KL ≥ NLL ≫ MSE), and **not τ=0.01** — that folklore is sharpening for unnormalized Q-value teachers; Kickstarting used softmax-policy teacher probs unmodified. Keep the entropy bonus: near-one-hot PPO teachers otherwise collapse student entropy and strand any later RL (Schmitt; Czarnecki on CE direction).
- **λ schedule:** one λ per teacher, start ~1.0 relative to the policy loss, exponential decay toward 0 over the run (QDagger "weaning"; Kickstarting's PBT is unnecessary — manual schedules work). Decaying to zero is also the mitigation for Czarnecki's mixed-objective oscillation mode; treat non-monotone per-cell curves during the mixed phase as expected.
- **Minibatch balance:** per-cell buffers, draws balanced **by cell/episode, not env steps** — otherwise 22000-step cells outweigh the tight cell ~7x per episode, re-importing the seesaw's suspected imbalance (Rusu/Actor-Mimic per-game memories).
- **Critic:** trains on env returns from step one alongside distillation (Kickstarting Eq. 3 — no teacher V needed); by the time λ is weaned the critic is calibrated, which deletes the fresh-critic collapse mode. Only if a pure-supervised phase is ever used anyway: critic-only warmup with policy frozen until value loss plateaus (bounded — overlong warmup overfits, ReinFlow), then unfreeze under a KL-to-distilled-self anchor (AlphaStar pattern).
- **Budget/targets:** Kickstarting matched from-scratch in ~10x fewer steps; expect ≪200M — plan 50-100M with the same per-cell probe battery. Calibration: ≥90% of each teacher's per-cell success under distillation, plausibly >100% with the RL term live. A cell at ≪90% is a routing/state-distribution bug, **not** capacity. Validate with full-episode rollouts per cell — the tight skill partly lives in recurrent state that per-step KL doesn't directly supervise.

Plumbing delta vs. current trainer: lockstep teacher inference during rollout collection + one CE term + per-teacher λ decay. No new infrastructure.

## 4. CAPACITY PROBE (runnable regardless of T13's outcome)

Three tiers, cheapest first; run tier 1 this week regardless.

1. **α-sweep interpolation** (zero training): θ(α) = (1−α)·θ_generalist_rungB + α·θ_tight_child, ~10 α values × ~200 eps × 6 cells. These checkpoints share an init (the tight child was fine-tuned *from* the generalist) — exactly the linear-mode-connectivity setting (Mirzadeh ICLR 2021), with Rewarded Soups (NeurIPS 2023) confirming LMC for RL fine-tunes. **Success is decisive**: one 128-net provably holds all 6 skills, and the best α is a better T13 warm start than the prepped endpoint. **Failure is only inconclusive** (two 50M rungs at 1e-2 may have left the basin) — a caveat, not a reason to skip a free experiment.
2. **Supervised mode-table fit** (the Karpathy overfit-a-batch discriminator, Ba & Caruana license): fit a fresh 128-hidden student to both teachers' action distributions on logged teacher rollouts, measure per-cell distribution-matching accuracy. High fit on all 6 = representational capacity is a measured fact and the fallback is pre-built; a tight-vs-wide tradeoff *under dense supervision* would be the first genuine evidence of a width-128 limit. (Claim is representational only — closed-loop success needs the student-driven recipe of §3.)
3. **Head-only retrain on the wreckage** (Davari CVPR 2022): take `t11t_remix_child.pt` (tight 0/200), freeze encoder+LSTM, retrain only heads on tight-cell data. Recovery = forgetting concentrated at the readout, trunk intact → low-LR consolidation/anchoring should suffice. No recovery = trunk overwritten → strengthens the anchor/distillation case even if T13 limps through.

Bonus framing from the practitioner angle: tier 2 is worth running **even if T13 succeeds** — it converts "probably not capacity" into a measurement, which is the kind of claim this project's docs are built on.

## 5. CONTRADICTIONS (flagged, not papered over)

1. **Does a KL anchor actually retain skills?** Wolczyk/CLEAR: output-space anchoring is *the* load-bearing fix. RFT continual paper (2507.05386): removing the explicit KL penalty caused *no additional forgetting* (only instability) — retention came from zero-advantage on-policy updates on mastered behavior. Partial resolution: different settings (LLM post-training vs warm-started control PPO), and — key — **your gamma=1 potential shaping keeps advantages nonzero on mastered cells, which disables the very zero-advantage shield the anti-anchor paper relies on.** In this project the anchor argument is *stronger* than in theirs. But the disagreement is real and the anchor should be treated as well-supported, not proven.
2. **Shaping cuts both ways, and the agents talk past it.** One agent: shaping invites drift on 97-100% cells (nonzero gradients forever). Same agent: degraded-cell recovery "must come from the dense shaping signal." And the project's own law says cells at ~0 never bootstrap *despite shaping being present*. Empirical reconciliation from your own table: shaping sufficed to recover cells from 6.5-40% (re-mix, at 1e-2) but never from ~0. The bootstrap threshold sits somewhere in (0, ~6.5%] — which is why E3_j2 at 6.5% is the cell to watch, and why "E3 flat at mid-run" is ambiguous between below-threshold, LR-starved, and plasticity-locked. The §2 telemetry is what disambiguates.
3. **Fallback architecture: two-phase vs single-phase.** The forgetting-rehearsal agent (following Czarnecki's oscillation result) prescribes pure supervised distillation first, RL polish strictly separate. The practitioner and multitask agents (following Kickstarting) prescribe single-phase joint RL+CE with weaning, and note pure-BC→RL handoff is inferior. Genuine literature tension. Adjudication: **single-phase wins here** — Czarnecki's oscillation manifested at 4k actions (you have 31) and is mitigated by weaning λ→0, while the two-phase handoff is precisely the transition that collapsed 4/4 times in R5 *in this codebase*. In-project evidence breaks the tie.
4. **Is the LR drop the fix or a delay?** Mirzadeh: LR is the single most influential forgetting knob. Ray Interference: interference lives in data-generation coupling — LR changes the timescale, not the attractor; if tight ever hits ~0, weight 0.25 will not bring it back. Both hold simultaneously: 1e-3 buys time and reduces per-update damage, but only monitoring + rollback (or an anchor) removes the absorbing state.
5. **The 25% number is softer than it looks.** Ibrahim's 25%-defends-strong-shift was *supervised replay with dense loss on every replayed token*. T13's 25% is on-policy sparse-reward rehearsal — a strictly weaker mechanism. The order of magnitude transfers; the guarantee does not.
6. **"Not capacity" has a scope limit.** The specialists' counter-evidence rules out *representational* capacity at width 128. It does not rule out (a) training-time gradient interference, which width also governs (Mirzadeh ICML 2022 — wider nets forget less, a lever unused here), or (b) *this lineage's* plasticity after multiple 1e-2 ladders (Dohare Nature 2024, Juliani & Ash — on-policy PPO specifically). A T13 plateau needs the §2 rank/dormancy check before any capacity conclusion.
7. **Advantage normalization:** Andrychowicz's mega-study found it has little average effect; VAPO/PopArt lines say it materially distorts under heterogeneous episode lengths. Not a true contradiction (its effect is task-dependent), but it means turning it off is licensed while its harm here is unproven — hence "log first, pre-register the amendment" rather than change tonight.

**Bottom line for tonight's relay:** launch T13 as prepped **plus** the probe battery, rollback rule, dense early checkpoints, and the logging in §2 (none touch the training design); add the tight-cell-only KL anchor if it's a same-day change, otherwise it's the first amendment; run the α-sweep and re-mix autopsy in parallel on eval capacity; and if T13 seesaws, the fallback is the single-phase kickstarting recipe in §3, not two-phase BC→polish.
